# GitHub social preview specification

Prepare the final social card manually after the repository screenshot and public release are verified.

## Canvas

- Size: 1280 × 640 px.
- Safe area: keep important text at least 80 px from every edge.
- Export: PNG, sRGB, under 1 MB when practical.

## Content

**Title:** Excel Data Toolbox AI

**Flow:** One prompt → Clean → Analyze → Validate → Deliver

**Subtitle:** Local-first Excel automation

Use a restrained white and deep-green palette that matches the application. Include one small workbook motif, not a screenshot collage. Avoid provider logos, stock photos, fake charts, API-key fields, and claims such as “all Excel” or “100% accurate.”

## Acceptance checklist

- Readable at 640 × 320 px.
- Product name and value proposition are clear in under five seconds.
- No customer data or private local path.
- No claim that exceeds the current README.
- Uploaded in **Settings → General → Social preview** only after final review.
